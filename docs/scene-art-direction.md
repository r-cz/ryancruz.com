# Illustrated Love Field scene

Implemented September 13, 2026 from Ryan’s approved desktop, portrait and night concepts. The visual system uses painted texture, simplified shapes, dark navy outlines, honey timber, blue sky, gray striped carpet, charcoal upholstery and silver seat frames. The cream laptop display, Georgia name, Inter supporting text and native HTML controls remain separate from the illustration.

The gate 20/Philadelphia/PHL and gate 18/Atlanta/ATL pair is intentionally illustrative. Seating faces both directions, with visible seat backs toward the windows. The skyline is one shared asset, preserving building order and profiles through layout and clock changes. No separate night or mobile city drawing replaces it. Airport geography and interior placement form a photo-informed composite, not a surveyed gate view.

## Asset construction

The built-in image generator produced separate production layers from the approved concepts and photographic references. Exact prompts are recorded in [scene-art-prompts.json](scene-art-prompts.json). The website does not display the approved concept as a flattened screenshot. The plane, clouds, sky, skyline, apron, terminal, seating, table and laptop shell are independent assets. Background art excludes moving objects and website controls. Gate labels and aircraft branding belong to the illustrated setting.

Production files are optimized WebP in `public/scene/`. ImageMagick was used for technical resizing, WebP encoding and trimming transparent sprite padding. Painted outlines and surfaces remain in the generated artwork. The terminal output retained an opaque matte in the window openings; measured SVG masks expose the outdoor layers while retaining columns, frames, door and podium. The skyline’s gray matte is keyed out with an SVG color-difference mask. These masks depend on the current asset geometry and palette; update them when replacing those assets.

Night uses the same artwork and positions, with CSS color/brightness weights. A second copy of the exact skyline selects warm window marks and green tower edges for restrained illumination. Small aligned taxiway lights fade in on the apron. This prevents geometry changes or shifting furniture as local time changes. Dawn/sunset are clock-based atmosphere rather than astronomical or weather calculations.

The laptop screen is blank in its image. Its real HTML text and link share the shell’s layout and transform origin, so text stays selectable and keyboard accessible. The desktop preview includes the name, role, introduction and action; portrait and short landscape views retain only name, role and action. On the smallest phones the laptop moves upward to keep the entry cue and motion control unobscured while remaining below the aircraft route.

## Maintenance

- Keep `src/scene-art.ts` asset revision aligned with artwork/layout changes to avoid stale cached dimensions.
- Preserve measured source dimensions for the terminal masks and gate crops. Keep gate screens in portrait proportion on phones.
- Preserve skyline geometry across all lighting states. Do not generate separate buildings for night or mobile.
- Verify independent motion, complete offscreen wrapping, pause/reduced motion, native links, essential-art fallback and disposal if altering the scene lifecycle.
- Keep the scene ambient and the portfolio content/routes intact.
