# Illustrated Love Field scene

Revised September 13, 2026 from Ryan’s feedback on the first illustrated scene. Painted texture, dark navy outlines, honey timber, blue sky, striped carpet, charcoal upholstery and silver frames remain. A black Patagonia Mini MLC backpack and a closed silver MacBook Pro replace the live laptop screen.

The backpack links to a prerendered travel journal with three “Coming soon” placeholders; the MacBook links to the existing portfolio below. Labels and a restrained edge pulse identify the native links. RC and “Skip to portfolio” have translucent blurred backings. The page uses ordinary scrolling and fixed object aspect ratios. Gate signs, zooming and the manual pause control were removed.

The MAX 8 includes dual-feather winglets, scalloped nacelle edges, continuous passenger windows behind the wordmark, and “8768 ETOPS” on the nose gear door. Small markings are present in the source artwork and naturally become subtle at scene scale.

Seating faces both directions, including seat backs toward the windows. The boarding entrance has a continuous raised floor and roof into a glass corridor that turns left and leaves the screen. That corridor follows Ryan’s description; its exact geometry was not verified against a surveyed gate or a specific photograph. Ground equipment combines reference vehicle types rather than asserting a current DAL fleet inventory. Airport geography and interior placement form a photo-informed composite.

## Asset construction

The built-in image generator produced separate production layers. Exact original prompts remain in [scene-art-prompts.json](scene-art-prompts.json), and the revision’s prompts and selected outputs are in [scene-art-prompts-v2.json](scene-art-prompts-v2.json).

The site composes sky, clouds, skyline, apron, aircraft, terminal, front/back seating, foreground seats, backpack, closed MacBook and three ground-vehicle sprites. Moving objects and website UI are excluded from background artwork. The new apron contains no stationary generated service vehicles.

Production files are optimized WebP in `public/scene/`. ImageMagick handled technical cropping, resizing and encoding. Alpha-channel inspection distinguished genuine transparency from generated checkerboards; aircraft and provisioning cutouts were regenerated to remove baked checkerboards.

The terminal uses solid magenta only in its open glass areas. An SVG color matrix keys out that matte while retaining opaque neutral metalwork, door hardware, columns, corridor floor and ceiling. Upper, middle and floor regions share the same 1536×1024 architecture asset. This replaces approximate window masks that left checkerboard traces around the pushbar. The skyline retains its existing color-difference key.

Night uses the same artwork and positions with CSS color/brightness weights. A second copy of the exact skyline selects warm window marks and green tower edges for restrained illumination. Taxiway lights fade in along the apron. Dawn and sunset are clock-based atmosphere, not astronomical or weather calculations. The travel banner reuses the same skyline and terminal art to preserve building order; it is static daylight and needs no client script.

The foreground seat group has a fixed 2:1 aspect ratio. Belongings preserve intrinsic image ratios and use percentage positions within that group. Resize logic only measures ambient routes; it never resizes a live screen, changes scroll progress, or transforms the portfolio.

## Maintenance

- Keep `src/scene-art.ts` asset revision aligned with artwork and intrinsic-dimension changes to avoid stale cached sprites.
- Preserve the terminal source regions and magenta key when replacing architecture.
- Preserve skyline geometry across lighting states, mobile layouts and the travel banner.
- Keep brand text facing correctly when a vehicle changes direction; do not mirror the entire sprite.
- Verify complete offscreen wrapping, reduced motion, native links/history, essential-art fallback and disposal if altering the scene lifecycle.
- Stop animation and object pulses while the scene is offscreen or the document is hidden. Reduced-motion visitors still receive minute-based clock refreshes.
- Keep the travel placeholders clearly unpublished and the existing portfolio content intact.
